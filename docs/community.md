# The RAGE fan community

OpenRAGE is the flagship project of a wider **Discord fan club for the RAGE franchise**. It's a
community for the games first. It's not only a development server.

## Purpose

- Keep enthusiasm for RAGE alive.
- Give fans a place to discuss the games and share their appreciation.
- Bring together fans, modders, preservation enthusiasts and people who simply enjoy the games.
- Welcome people who can't contribute code as well as technical contributors.

## Games

- **RAGE (2011)** is the heart of the club.
- **RAGE 2** is welcome. People can discuss and appreciate it without it needing equal focus,
  and nobody has to agree on which game is better.

## Possible activities

- Lore and story discussions
- Favorite characters and locations
- Nostalgia and first-playthrough memories
- Screenshots and gameplay moments
- Fan art
- Mod showcases and modding discussion
- Technical discussion of the original megatexture system
- OpenRAGE development updates, testing, bug reports and documentation
- Welcoming newcomers to the games

## Server style (open decision)

- **Wasteland-themed:** channels named after places such as Wellspring and Subway Town.
- **Conventional:** a standard gaming-community layout with RAGE aesthetics.
